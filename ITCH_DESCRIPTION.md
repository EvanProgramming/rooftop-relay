# Rooftop Relay

Carry three forgotten letters across Shenzhen's neon rooftops. Run, leap between buildings, and dodge the spinning ventilation fans before delivering everything to the glowing mailbox.

Controls: Left/Right or A/D to move; Space, W, or Up to jump. Collect all three letters to unlock the mailbox. You have three hearts and a checkpoint. Press R to retry after a lost run.
